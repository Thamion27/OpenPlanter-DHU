# CIB-8 Red-Team QC

## Purpose
Attack the investigation before outside reality does.

## QC Questions

```text
1. What claim is overstated?
2. What evidence is weakest?
3. What alternative explanation fits the facts?
4. What source might be biased, stale, incomplete, or misunderstood?
5. What would disprove the main finding?
6. What action would be irresponsible before further verification?
7. Are any legal, ethical, or dignity boundaries being approached?
```

```text
Reason:
Required corrections:

```
